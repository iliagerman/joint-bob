The tour

All of it in 50 seconds

It runs where you put it

Your machines

It installs as your own user on macOS, Linux, or Windows with WSL 2: a laptop, a box under the desk, an EC2 instance. One machine is enough to start.

Your agents

Claude Code, Pi and Kiro, signed in with your own accounts. Pick the agent, model and reasoning level for each conversation.

Your network

It serves on localhost. Add Tailscale, your own HTTPS, or a relay when you want your phone and your other machines in.

Install

One command, on a machine you own

No sudo. The installer downloads the latest release, checks its checksum and sets up a service for your user. Then open the local address it prints and create your account.
