What changed and why

Decisions in this mock

  • Six quick actions at the top: Mark done, New note, New conversation, Add secret, Auto respond, Back to conversations. Each is a single tap, with no nested menu.
  • Mark done is full width and tinted, as before, because it is the most frequent action. The label flips to "Mark not done" after a tap.
  • Auto respond shows its state on the tile (ON or OFF), so you can see it without opening anything.
  • Run settings become a 2×2 summary (agent, model, effort, node). The values are readable at a glance. Tapping a chip opens the same picker as today.
  • Lower-frequency navigation is grouped under More: Tools, Recent, Running, and Needs review. The Needs review count stays visible.
  • Footer keeps three items: All projects, Costs, and Settings. Conversations moved into the Back tile, so it no longer appears twice.

Open questions for you

  • Add secret: should it open the secret-account form directly, or the Settings secrets page? The mock assumes the direct form.
  • Order: is New note or New conversation more frequent? The mock keeps your order from the request.
  • Outside chat: the Projects and Board views have their own actions. Should the same quick-action grid appear there, or only in a conversation?
  • Customisation: should you be able to reorder or hide quick actions later, or is a fixed list enough for now?
